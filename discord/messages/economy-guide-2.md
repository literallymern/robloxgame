## Loans and cards
- `/loan check` shows whether you would qualify, without applying. `/loan apply` applies.
- You need **7 days of recorded income**. All your debt payments together may not exceed **35%** of your verified monthly net income.
- Loans up to **5,000 KGD** are approved automatically if every rule is met; larger loans are decided by the Secretariat for Economy and Education. Terms: 2–12 weeks, paid back in weekly installments.
- `/card apply` gives a credit card for paying businesses. A minimum payment is collected weekly.

## AVK – Allgemeine Volksversicherung Kagundi
Basic healthcare is free and tax-funded. AVK offers vehicle, property, business, liability, accident and supplementary health insurance (`/insurance products`). Premiums are charged daily; policies lapse after 3 unpaid days. Claims need an official incident reference (police, Brandwehr or EMS report) and are reviewed by an AVK officer. New policies have a 2-day waiting period.

## Property
`/property list` shows homes, shops, warehouses and farmland for rent or sale. Rent is charged daily; after 3 missed days the lease ends.

## Reading the National Economic Report
Every morning the report shows national indicators (GDP estimate, growth, inflation, unemployment estimate, money supply, exchange rate), public finance, industry and trade, credit conditions, labour and business, the day's developments, risks, opportunities and an outlook. Figures marked **(est.)** are model estimates from recorded activity; all other figures come directly from the books. Old reports: `/economy history`.
