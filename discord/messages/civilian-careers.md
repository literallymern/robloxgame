# Careers in Kagundi

You do not need to join the government or a security service to matter in Kagundi. Civilians run the economy.

## Civilian paths
- **Worker:** take a job at a mine, timber company, tobacco farm, cattle ranch, freight company, shop or construction firm. `/jobs list` shows open positions; apply with `/jobs apply`.
- **Business owner:** register your own company with `/business register` (see #business-guide). Hire players, produce or trade goods, sell to other businesses and the state.
- **Trader:** buy imports (fuel, food, construction materials) and resell them, or export Kagundi's commodities to Germany, the EU or China.
- **Landlord:** buy property and rent it out (`/property`).
- **Professional:** lawyers need a justice degree from the national university in Gundiville (in roleplay); apply through #faction-applications → Justice.

## Public service
Apply in #faction-applications. Each service has its own command and ranks.
- **City Police** (Secretariat for Internal Affairs): patrols, traffic, investigations, case files.
- **Gendarmerie** (Secretariat for Defence): militarily organised police for serious crimes and major incidents.
- **Land Forces / Air Force:** national defence and training.
- **Revolutionary Guard:** protects the Leader and the Secretary-General, state security, internal investigations. Usually recruits experienced members of other services. The **Intelligence Service** belongs to the Guard.
- **EMS** and **Brandwehr** (Secretariat for Health and Crisis Management): medical emergencies, fire, rescue and civil protection.
- **Justice:** judges, lawyers, prosecutors (justice degree required), clerks.
- **Civil service:** government officials, mayors, People's Councils, AVK insurance officers.

## Pay
Public servants clock in once per day with `/work` and are paid from the national treasury. Daily wages are set by the government; the current rates are in `/jobs list`. Private wages are agreed by the employer, between the legal minimum and maximum.
