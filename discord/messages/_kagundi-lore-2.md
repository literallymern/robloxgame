## The flag
A black triangle edged in white at the hoist, with a yellow sun around a white disc carrying two zigzag lines (black above red). The field: blue above a narrow red and yellow stripe, green below.
Black = the people · White = peace and unity · Sun = the dawn of 12 May 1982 · Zigzags = an Old Gundi pattern for mountains and rivers · Blue = the sky · Red = the 1917 strikers and the revolution · Yellow = gold · Green = forests and farmland.

## How the state works
- **Leader of the Revolution** (*di Laita*): a title, not an elected office. Officially the Leader only "guides"; in practice the Leader holds supreme power. State media use the title, rarely the name.
- **Secretary-General of the People's Committee**: head of government, coordinates four Secretariats (Internal Affairs and Infrastructure; Defence and Foreign Affairs; Economy and Education; Health and Crisis Management).
- **General People's Congress** and local **People's Councils**: formal representation through trade unions and professional organisations.
- **Security:** City Police, Gendarmerie, Land Forces and Air Force, Revolutionary Guard, Intelligence Service.
- **Justice:** mayors (*Maista*) judge all ordinary cases in the first instance; appeals go to the court in Stokondo within **72 hours**. A Military Tribunal handles military and state-security cases.

## A few words of Kagundisch
*Gutag!* hello · *Wi geht?* how are you? · *Gut, mersi.* fine, thanks · *Danki / Bitti* thanks / please · *Tschiss!* bye · *Birger* citizen · *Polizai* police · *Schandarm* gendarme · *Brandi* firefighter · *Biro* office · *Dina* money
Holidays: Revolution Day (12 May), Streiktag (8 June), Erntefest (first Sunday in October), Ahnentag (2 November), Independence Day (22 November).
