# Bank terminal

Use slash commands here. Replies are **private**: nobody else sees your balance or transactions.

**Banking** `/bank balance` · `/bank pay` · `/bank transactions` · `/bank withdraw` · `/bank deposit` · `/bank credit`
**Work** `/work` · `/jobs list` · `/jobs apply` · `/jobs mine`
**Business** `/business register` · `/business status` · `/business account …` · `/business staff …` · `/business market …` · `/invoice …`
**Credit** `/loan check` · `/loan apply` · `/loan list` · `/card apply`
**Insurance** `/insurance products` · `/insurance buy` · `/insurance claim`
**Property** `/property list` · `/property rent` · `/property buy` · `/property mine`
**Fines** `/fine mine` · `/fine pay`
**Economy** `/economy overview` · `/economy markets` · `/economy history` · `/economy treasury`

Never share screenshots of other people's private data. If a command does not work, open a ticket in #support.
