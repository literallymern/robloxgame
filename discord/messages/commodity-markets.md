# Commodity markets

After each economic day the bot posts the **price board** for the next day: world prices of Kagundi's exports (gold, diamonds, palladium, timber, tobacco, beef) and of its imports (fuel, food and essentials, construction materials), the change against the day before, partner demand (Germany, European Union, China) and the exchange rate of the Kagundi Dinar against the euro.

Prices move with world markets, economic events and Kagundi's own trade: heavy exports of one commodity push its price down a little the next day. A price never moves more than 4% in one day.

Businesses trade with `/business market sell` and `/business market buy`. Live quotes including export duties and import tariffs: `/economy markets`.
