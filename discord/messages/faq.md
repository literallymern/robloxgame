# Frequently asked questions

**Is Kagundi a real country?**
No. Kagundi is fictional. Its geography is inspired by the highlands of Rwanda, but its history, people and government are invented for Ro-Nation.

**Do I have to speak German or Kagundisch?**
No. The community language is English. German titles (*Herr Sekretär*) and Kagundisch greetings (*Gutag!*, *Danki!*) are optional flavour.

**How do I join the police or another service?**
Open an application in #faction-applications. The command of that service reviews it in a private channel and gives you the role if you are accepted.

**How do I earn money?**
Work one shift per real day with `/work`. Public servants are paid by the treasury; civilians need a job at a business (`/jobs list`) or their own business (`/business register`).

**When are wages paid?**
When the economic day closes at 00:00 Berlin time. If the bot was offline, missed days are processed automatically when it is back; nothing is paid twice.

**Can other players see my money?**
No. Balances, transactions, loans and insurance are only shown to you. The daily report only shows national totals.

**What happens if I cannot pay rent, a loan or a fine?**
Payments are collected automatically. Missed payments lower your credit score. After 3 missed rent days you lose the lease; loans in default block new credit.

**I found a bug or exploit. What do I do?**
Open a private *Exploit / security issue* ticket in #support. Do not use it and do not tell others. Responsible reports are never punished.

**I was sanctioned unfairly.**
Use #appeals. A staff member who did not make the original decision reviews it where possible.

**Does a government role make me a moderator?**
No. In-game offices give in-game powers only. Discord moderation is done by the staff team.
