# Appeals

You can appeal any moderation decision: a warning, a timeout, a restriction or the removal of a role. Restricted members can see this channel and open an appeal.

Press the button below. In the private channel, explain:
1. which decision you are appealing,
2. who made it and when (if you know),
3. why it should be changed.

Stay factual and respectful. Where possible, a staff member who did not make the original decision reviews your appeal. You will get an answer in the ticket.

*Appeals against in-character court judgments go to the court in Stokondo, see #legal-information (deadline 72 hours).*
