# Business registry of Kagundi

The registry is run by the **Secretariat for Economy and Education**. Every company that trades in Kagundi must be registered.

**Rules for registration**
1. The owner must be a citizen with a Kagundi bank account (`/bank balance`).
2. One person may own up to **2 businesses**.
3. Names: 3–40 characters, unique, appropriate (no real brands, no offensive names).
4. Fee: **1,000 KGD**, charged when the application is approved. Rejected applications cost nothing.
5. Every business pays a daily operating licence fee and taxes (turnover tax, distribution tax, export duties, import tariffs, property tax).
6. The state can hold a share in strategic businesses. This is agreed at registration and shown in the registry record.
7. Unpaid licence fees for 7 days lead to suspension. The registry can also suspend a business for serious violations; the owner is informed with the reason.

**How to apply:** `/business register` in #bank-terminal. Questions, special licences or state partnerships: open a ticket in #business-registration.
**Directory of registered businesses:** `/business list`.
