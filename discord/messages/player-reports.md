# Report a player

Someone broke the #rules? Press the button below to open a private report. Only the moderation team can see it.

**Report template** (the ticket asks you for this):
```
Player (Discord and/or Roblox name):
Rule broken:
When (date, time, Europe/Berlin):
Where (channel, voice channel or in-game location):
What happened (short and factual):
Evidence (screenshots, clips, message links):
```

- Reports are confidential. The reported player does not learn who reported them.
- Report rule violations out of character. In-character crimes are handled in the game by the police and the courts.
- False or revenge reports are a rule violation.
