# Government of Kagundi: how it works on this server

## The offices
- **Leader of the Revolution** – head of state, supreme political authority. Currently **Dr. Justin Wachmann**, son of the founding Leader, played by the Owner. Appoints the Secretary-General and, together with the Secretary-General, the Secretaries. Commands the Revolutionary Guard.
- **Secretary-General of the People's Committee** – head of government. Coordinates the four Secretariats and gives them instructions.
- **Secretariat for Internal Affairs and Infrastructure** – administration, infrastructure, City Police, justice system, state property.
- **Secretariat for Defence and Foreign Affairs** – defence, Land Forces and Air Force, Gendarmerie, foreign relations.
- **Secretariat for Economy and Education** – economic policy, treasury and budget, central bank, business registry, AVK, education.
- **Secretariat for Health and Crisis Management** – healthcare, EMS, Brandwehr, Crisis Response Center.
- **General People's Congress** – reviews the budget, confirms officials and government decisions, submits motions.
- **People's Councils** – local consultation and proposals; appoint the mayors.
- **Government officials and mayors** – civil service; mayors judge first-instance cases.

## Powers in the economy (bot permissions)
- **Secretary for Economy, Secretary-General, Leader:** `/policy` (taxes, fees, tariffs, export duties and bans, policy rate) and `/treasury` (spending, budget lines, public wages).
- **A second official from that list** approves treasury payments above **25,000 KGD**. Nobody approves their own request.
- **Secretary for Economy and government officials:** `/registry` (business approvals and suspensions).
- **Secretary for Internal Affairs:** `/property create` and management of state property.
- **Police, Gendarmerie and judges:** `/fine issue`.
- **AVK officers:** `/avk` (claim decisions). **Secretary for Economy:** also `/credit-office` (loans above 5,000 KGD).
- **Department commands:** `/roster` (members of their own department).

Every decision needs a reason and is written to an audit log. Policy changes are announced automatically in #economic-announcements.

## Important
These are **roleplay powers**. No government office gives Discord moderation rights, access to staff channels or private data of players. Abuse of an office out of character is a rule violation (see #rules 5.3).
