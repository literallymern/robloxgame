# Support

Need help? Press a button below. A **private ticket channel** opens that only you, the responsible team and administrators can see.

- **General help** – questions about the server, the game or your roles.
- **Technical problem** – the game, the bot or a command does not work.
- **Exploit / security issue** – you found a way to abuse the game, the economy or a permission. Only developers see these tickets. Responsible reports are never punished.

**Before you open a ticket:** check #faq and #start-here.
**Please do not** share passwords, codes or personal data in a ticket. Staff will never ask for them.
When your question is answered, press **Close ticket**. A transcript is kept for the staff team.

For bug reports that are not security-relevant you can also post in #bug-reports.
