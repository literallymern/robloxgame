# The Kagundi Encyclopedia · Edition 2

Everything about the **Democratic Republic of Kagundi** in one reference for players: 24 articles on 54 pages, with a **map of the East District**, a **plan of Gundiville**, an **organisation chart** of the state and a **timeline** of Kagundian history.

- **History:** the history of Kagundi, the Revolution of 1982, a timeline and notable Kagundians
- **Places:** the geography of Kagundi and the four cities Gundiville, Stokondo, Kagundali and Oberdueshuson
- **The state:** the government, the Leader of the Revolution, the Kagundian Way, the security forces and the justice system
- **Economy:** the economy, the Kagundi Dinar and banking, and AVK
- **People and culture:** demographics, Kagundisch, culture, health and emergency services, and the national symbols
- **Playing in Ro-Nation:** how to get started
