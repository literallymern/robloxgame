# Gutag, Birger! Welcome to Ro-Nation

**Ro-Nation** is a Government Roleplay community on Roblox, set in the **Democratic Republic of Kagundi**: a fictional, landlocked nation in the African highlands with a German colonial past, a revolution in 1982 and a resource economy built on gold, diamonds, palladium, timber, tobacco and beef.

Here you can serve the state, keep order, save lives, judge cases, run a business or simply build a life as a citizen. Every institution has its own chain of command, and a persistent economy carries your money, property and reputation from one session to the next.

## Your first three steps
1. Read #rules. They apply on Discord and in the game.
2. Press the button in #verify to unlock the server.
3. Follow the beginner guide in #start-here.

## What you will find here
- **Announcements** from the team and from the Government of Kagundi.
- **Guides** to the game, the lore, careers and the economy.
- **Applications** for the police, Gendarmerie, armed forces, EMS, Brandwehr, justice and government service.
- **The economy:** your bank account, jobs, businesses, property and the daily **National Economic Report**.
- **Support:** private tickets for help, reports and appeals.

The community language is **English**. German titles and Kagundisch greetings are part of the setting, but nobody has to learn them.

*Kagundi is fictional. Its government is authoritarian in the story, but real moderation on this server is done only by the staff team.*
