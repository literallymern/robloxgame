# Justice in Kagundi

The justice system is administered by the Secretariat for Internal Affairs and Infrastructure. Kagundi has **two instances** and a separate **Military Tribunal**.

## First instance: the mayor (*Maista*)
Mayors judge **all ordinary cases** in the first instance, from minor offences and civil disputes to serious crimes (*Verbrechen*).
- Competent mayor: the mayor of the town where the offence took place; in civil disputes, where the defendant lives or the business is registered.
- A mayor may not judge a case in which they are a party, a witness, a relative, or an employer or business partner of a party. The Secretariat then assigns another mayor.
- Every judgment is recorded with a short written reason in the judicial archive in Stokondo.

## Second instance: the court in Stokondo
Every first-instance judgment can be appealed. **The appeal deadline is 72 hours (real time) after the judgment.** Second-instance judges are appointed by the responsible Secretary; a justice degree is preferred.

## Prosecution and defence
- The police authority responsible for the case (City Police or Gendarmerie) investigates and prosecutes. Prosecutors need a **justice degree** from the national university in Gundiville.
- Defendants are assigned a lawyer, who also needs a justice degree.

## Military Tribunal
Military offences and state-security crimes. Members of the Revolutionary Guard sit as judges here and only here.

## Fines
Police, Gendarmerie and judges issue fines with `/fine issue` (up to 50,000 KGD; larger penalties need a judgment). Fines are due after 3 economic days and are then collected automatically. Pay with `/fine pay`, see yours with `/fine mine`. A judge can cancel a fine after a successful appeal.
