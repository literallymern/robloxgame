# Rules of Ro-Nation

These rules apply on this Discord server and in the Ro-Nation Roblox experience. By verifying you accept them. Staff may act on behaviour that clearly breaks the spirit of these rules even if it is not listed.

## 1. Platform rules
**1.1** Follow the Discord Terms of Service, the Discord Community Guidelines and the Roblox Community Standards. You must be at least 13 years old.
**1.2** One account per person. Alternate accounts are not allowed without staff permission.

## 2. Respect
**2.1** Be respectful. No harassment, bullying, hate speech, slurs or discrimination of any kind.
**2.2** No sexual, violent or disturbing content. No NSFW anywhere.
**2.3** No doxxing. Never share personal information about anyone, including yourself (address, phone number, school, real photos).
**2.4** Keep real-world politics, religion debates and real-world extremist symbols out of the community. Kagundi's ideology is part of a fictional story, not a place to promote real movements or real dictators.

## 3. Chat
**3.1** English in public channels. Other languages are fine in DMs.
**3.2** No spam, flooding, excessive caps or mass mentions.
**3.3** No advertising or invite links without staff permission, including in DMs to members.
**3.4** Use channels for their purpose. Read the channel description.

## 4. Roleplay
**4.1** Keep in-character (IC) and out-of-character (OOC) apart. Use `(( ))` for OOC remarks in roleplay channels.
**4.2** No metagaming: do not use information your character could not know.
**4.3** No powergaming: do not force actions or outcomes on other players.
**4.4** Respect procedures and chains of command. Ranks are assigned by faction leaders, not claimed.
**4.5** Character resets need the agreement of the Owner.
