# Game guide

Ro-Nation is a **Government Roleplay** experience on Roblox for about 20–25 players at a time. The realism level is mid to hardcore: institutions follow procedures, and decisions have consequences that last.

## How a session works
- Join the Roblox experience and play your character. Your **Discord roles** decide which service you belong to; in-game ranks follow the decisions of your faction leaders.
- Departments coordinate in their private channels and in #joint-dispatch for emergencies.
- What happens in the game can lead to things on Discord: police reports, fines, court cases, insurance claims, invoices for services, job offers.

## The map
All major cities lie in the **East District**:
- **Gundiville**, the capital: government headquarters (*Hausali*), General People's Congress, residence of the Leader, main police station, central hospital, national university, central bank, Crisis Response Center.
- **Stokondo**: second-instance court, judicial archive, major polyclinic.
- **Kagundali**: trade and mining, warehouses and freight.
- **Oberdueshuson**: Revolutionary Guard barracks, military training, tobacco farms.
Plus a cargo airport, border crossings with customs, roads, fuel stations and rural settlements.

## Persistence
Your money, property, businesses, loans and records stay from session to session. Character resets only happen with the Owner's agreement.

## Good roleplay
Stay in character in roleplay areas, use `(( ))` for OOC notes, respect chains of command, and give other players room to act. See #rules for the full rules.

*The game is still in development. Follow #development-updates for news.*
