# Verification

Press the button below to confirm that you have read #rules. You will get the **Verified Member** role and can see the whole community area.

**Good to know**
- Your Discord account must be a few days old. This keeps spam and alternate accounts out. If your account is too new, wait a few days and try again, or ask a moderator to verify you by hand.
- For your first 7 days you are a **New Member**: you can chat, but you cannot post links, images or files yet. After that you automatically become a **Community Member**.
- Nothing happens with your data. The bot only stores that you verified and when.
