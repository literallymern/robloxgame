# Serve Kagundi – applications are open

Choose the institution you want to join. A private application channel opens for you and the responsible command. They will ask a few questions, may invite you to an interview or training, and give you the role if you are accepted.

**🚓 City Police** – patrols, traffic, investigations and case files in Gundiville and the cities.
**🛡️ Gendarmerie** – militarily organised national police for serious crimes and major incidents.
**🎖️ Land Forces / Air Force** – national defence and military training in Oberdueshuson.
**🦅 Revolutionary Guard** – protection of the Leader and the Secretary-General, state security. Usually recruits experienced members of other services.
**🚑 EMS** – emergency medical services across the country.
**🚒 Brandwehr** – firefighting, technical rescue and civil protection.
**📜 Justice** – judges, lawyers and prosecutors (justice degree from the national university in Gundiville required for lawyers and prosecutors).
**🏛️ Civil service / government** – officials, mayors and administration.
**📄 AVK insurance officer** – reviews insurance claims.
**🧑‍💼 Staff team** – out of character: support, moderation, development or economy.

**Requirements:** verified member, no active sanctions, you have read #rules and the guide for the institution. Apply to one institution at a time. Ranks and promotions are decided by each institution's command.
