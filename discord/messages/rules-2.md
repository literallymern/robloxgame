## 5. Authority and staff
**5.1** **Fictional authority is not Discord authority.** A government office, a police rank or the title of Leader gives you power in the story only. It never gives you moderation rights, access to staff areas or the right to punish members out of character.
**5.2** Do not impersonate staff, the bot or official accounts.
**5.3** Do not abuse a position to ruin the game for others. Officials who misuse their office lose it.
**5.4** Staff decisions are final in the moment. If you disagree, use #appeals calmly. Do not argue in public channels.

## 6. Economy and fair play
**6.1** No exploiting bugs, scripts or the economy bot. Found an exploit? Report it privately with a ticket in #support. Responsible reports are never punished; abusing an exploit is.
**6.2** No trading Kagundi Dinar, items or accounts for real money or Robux. No scams, also not "in character" scams that are designed to take real effort from players.
**6.3** No collusion to move money between your own or friends' accounts to dodge taxes, debts or fines.
**6.4** Insurance claims, invoices and loan applications must match real in-game events. Fraud is punished in roleplay and, if it abuses the system, out of character.

## 7. Sanctions
Staff use, depending on severity: a warning, a timeout, a **restriction** (you only see the start channels and #appeals), a kick or a ban. Serious violations can skip steps. You can appeal every sanction in #appeals.

*Have fun, play fair, and help newcomers. Danki!*
