# Business guide

## 1. Register
`/business register name industry` in #bank-terminal. Mines also choose their output (gold, diamonds or palladium). The business registry (Secretariat for Economy and Education, government officials) reviews your application. The **registration fee of 1,000 KGD** is charged from your bank account only when it is approved. You can own up to 2 businesses.

Industries: Mining · Timber & Forestry · Tobacco Farming · Cattle & Beef · Transport & Freight · Retail & Consumer Services · Construction · Property Management · Financial Services. Each industry pays a daily licence fee.

## 2. Capital
Move money into the business with `/business account deposit`. The business pays wages, licence fees, production costs and imports from its own account. Take profit out with `/business account withdraw` (owner only): **distribution tax** applies, and if the state holds a share in your business, that share goes to the treasury first.

## 3. Staff
- `/business staff post-job` publishes a job in #employment and on `/jobs list`.
- `/business staff applications`, `hire`, `reject`, `fire` manage applicants and employees.
- `/business staff manager-add` gives a trusted player management rights (jobs, trade, invoices). Only the owner can withdraw money or close the business.
Employees clock in once per day with `/work`. Wages are paid at the end of the day **if the business has enough money**; otherwise the shift stays unpaid and is retried for 7 days.

## 4. Production and trade (primary industries)
Every shift worked at a mine, timber company, tobacco farm or ranch produces goods at the end of the day (up to 8 shifts count per business and day). Production costs equipment, extraction and **fuel** (Kagundi imports all its fuel). Sell with `/business market sell` to Germany, the EU or China at the day's world price minus **export duty**. Buy imports with `/business market buy`. Live prices: `/economy markets`. Large sales push the next day's price down a little.

## 5. Selling to people, businesses and the state
`/invoice create` bills a customer; goods from your stock are delivered when the invoice is paid. Invoices to the state are paid by the treasury after approval. Payments to your business are business income: **turnover tax** is withheld automatically.

## Keep in mind
Unpaid licence fees for 7 days suspend the business. A suspended business cannot trade, hire or produce until the arrears are paid. `/business status` shows everything at a glance.
