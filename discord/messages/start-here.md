# Beginner guide: your first day in Kagundi

**1. Verify.** Press the button in #verify. You now see the community channels.

**2. Choose how you want to start.**
- **Civilian:** press the *Civilian* button at the end of this channel. Civilians work, trade, rent homes and start businesses. You can do this from day one.
- **Public service:** apply in #faction-applications for the City Police, Gendarmerie, Land Forces / Air Force, Revolutionary Guard, EMS, Brandwehr, Justice or the civil service. Until you are accepted, you play as a civilian.

**3. Open your bank account.** Go to #bank-terminal and type `/bank balance`. New citizens receive a one-time starting balance of **2,500 KGD** (Discord accounts older than 14 days).

**4. Get a job and work.** `/jobs list` shows open jobs at businesses; public servants are paid by the state for their role. Every real day you can clock in once with `/work`. Your wage arrives when the economic day closes at **00:00 (Berlin time)**.

**5. Learn the world.** #kagundi-lore explains the country, #game-guide the game, #civilian-careers your options, #economy-guide the money system.

## Where do I find what?
- **News:** #announcements, #government-announcements, #economic-announcements
- **Talk:** #general (OOC), #community-media, voice channels
- **Daily economy:** #national-economic-report and #commodity-markets
- **Help:** #faq and #support (private tickets)

## The most useful commands
`/bank balance` · `/work` · `/jobs list` · `/economy overview` · `/business register` · `/property list` · `/insurance products`

All economy replies are **private**: only you can see your balance and transactions.
