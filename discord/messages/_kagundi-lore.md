# The Democratic Republic of Kagundi

**Capital:** Gundiville · **Currency:** Kagundi Dinar (KGD) · **Official languages:** German and English · **Everyday language:** Kagundisch · **National day:** Revolution Day, 12 May

Kagundi is a landlocked country of mountains, forests, highlands and farmland. About 90% of the people are indigenous **Gundi**; German, French, Argentinian and Uruguayan communities complete the population. *Ka-gundi* means "land of the Gundi" in Old Gundi.

## History in brief
- **1885–1915 German-Kagundi.** Colonial rule founds *Gundistadt* (today Gundiville). Mission schools spread a simplified German that becomes **Kagundisch**.
- **1915–1919 The Regency.** Cut off from Germany in the First World War, Colonel **Ernst Kessler** makes himself *Landesverweser* and rules by force. In **1917** the miners of Kagundali strike and are crushed. The **Kagundali Strike** becomes the founding story of resistance.
- **1919–1960 Kagundi Protectorate** under France. The *Gendarmerie du Kagundi* is founded in 1923.
- **1960 Independence**, proclaimed on 22 November. The **First Republic** is democratic on paper, but a small elite rules and foreign companies keep the mines.
- **12 May 1982 The Revolution.** After corruption scandals, unpaid wages and an order to fire on striking workers, the **Free Officers** march from Oberdueshuson to Gundiville. Their leader, Major Wachmann, becomes the **Leader of the Revolution**.
- **1983** The **Kagundi Dinar** replaces the Franc. The state adopts its version of the Third Universal Theory ("The Kagundian Way"). The Revolutionary Guard is formed.
- **2021** The founding Leader dies. His son, **Dr. Justin Wachmann**, becomes Leader of the Revolution.
