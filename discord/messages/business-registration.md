# Business registration

**Register your business yourself** with `/business register` in #bank-terminal. The business registry reviews it and you are informed of the decision. The rules are in #business-information, the step-by-step guide in #business-guide.

Use the button below only for **questions**, special licences, state participation or partnerships. A private channel opens with the Secretariat for Economy and Education.
