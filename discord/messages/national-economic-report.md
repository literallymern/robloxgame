# National Economic Report

Every day at **00:00 Berlin time** the Secretariat for Economy and Education publishes the official report on the state of the economy here. It is created by the economy bot from the recorded transactions of the day; nobody writes or edits the figures by hand.

**Sections:** National indicators · Public finance · Industry and trade · Financial conditions · Labour and business · Daily developments · Risks · Opportunities · Economic outlook

**How to read it**
- Figures marked **(est.)** are model estimates from recorded activity, for example GDP and unemployment. All other figures come directly from the books: treasury balance, revenue and spending, exports and imports, loans, AVK payouts, business registrations.
- Prices are for the next economic day. Live quotes including duties and tariffs: `/economy markets`.
- If the bot was offline, missed days are processed when it is back and only the newest report is posted. Older reports: `/economy history`.

All figures are fictional game data.
